How to read this
  • Absolute risk of death from any cause — not relative risk, not HF-specific.
  • The threshold is a statistical operating point, not a treatment threshold.
  • Source cohort: 4.2% died by 30 days, 22.6% by one year. A similar result is typical, not reassuring.
  • Below threshold is not a safety guarantee — most deaths occur in patients who were not flagged.
  • Blank fields fall back to the training median, as in development.
Why medications appear here

Beta-blocker and loop diuretic are among the strongest predictors, but they are prognostic markers, not causal levers — non-prescription usually reflects a judgement that the patient could not tolerate the drug. Toggling them does not estimate the effect of prescribing.

Model and validation

Elastic-net logistic regression on ten SHAP-ranked predictors. Developed in three Singapore hospitals, validated in five independent ones (n=5,016). Predominantly HFrEF; performance in HFpEF and outside Singapore is untested.
