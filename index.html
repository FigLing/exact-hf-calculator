EXACT-HF · ten-variable model

Mortality risk after heart failure hospitalisation

Estimates 30-day and 1-year all-cause mortality from ten variables recorded in routine care. Developed in three Singapore hospitals and validated in five independent ones.

Research use only. This model has not been prospectively or interventionally validated, and is not a medical device. It must not be used alone to guide care for an individual patient. It was developed in a predominantly HFrEF, multi-ethnic Singaporean cohort; performance elsewhere, and in HFpEF, is untested. Nothing entered here is stored, transmitted or recorded — this page is not a patient record, and a screenshot of it is not documentation.

How to read this

  • These are absolute risks of death from any cause, not relative risks and not heart-failure-specific mortality.
  • The threshold is the model's Youden point — a statistical operating point that balances sensitivity against specificity. It is not a treatment threshold, and crossing it does not by itself indicate any particular action.
  • Compare against the source cohort: 4.2% died within 30 days and 22.6% within a year. A result near those numbers is typical, not reassuring.
  • "Below threshold" is not a safety guarantee. Most deaths in absolute number still occur in patients who were not flagged.
  • The estimate describes patients resembling this one. It cannot tell you what will happen to this individual.

External validation

Elastic-net logistic regression on ten SHAP-ranked predictors. Coefficients, imputation medians, winsorising bounds and scaler constants are generated by step28_export_calculator_model.py and shipped in model.json — the page computes entirely in your browser and sends nothing anywhere.

Reference event rates in the source cohort: 30-day 4.2%, 1-year 22.6%. Risk bands use each model's Youden threshold from external validation.
